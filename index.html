A coordinated, place-based strategy to strengthen middle school math outcomes for grades 4–8, built on evidence-based practice.

who's behind it

A three-way partnership

M&M represents a deliberate shift from isolated interventions to a coordinated, ecosystem-level strategy. Starting June 2026, SMU Simmons brings together three centers to implement and evaluate Year 1 of the initiative in LancasterISD, in partnership with the United Way of Metropolitan Dallas.

RME

Research in Mathematics Education leads evidence-based math instruction across grades 4–8, guiding co-design of interventions like FFO and PAR.

TBC

The Budd Center serves as the main liaison to LancasterISD, leading family and community engagement efforts.

CORE

The Addy Foundation Center leads operations, data collection, and reporting across the full project.

the learning progression

From fractions to algebra readiness

Strong fraction understanding in the elementary grades provides the foundation for proportional reasoning and algebra readiness in middle school.

Grades 4–5

Fraction Face-Off

36 lessons in clusters, building fraction fluency through training, relay practice, timed sprints, and individual contests.

→
Grades 6–8

Promoting Algebra Readiness

Four strands, 96 lessons total, moving from whole number foundations through fraction operations to algebra readiness.

our shared language

Six evidence-based practices

Identified by the IES Practice Guide, these strong-evidence practices anchor every lesson, every coaching conversation, and every family resource across the project.

1

Systematic Instruction

Develop student understanding through a deliberate learning sequence.

2

Mathematical Language

Teach precise language so students can explain and defend their thinking.

3

Representations

Use a well-chosen set of concrete and semi-concrete models to build concepts.

4

Number Lines

Build understanding of magnitude and prepare students for advanced math.

5

Word Problems

Teach students to recognize problem structure rather than keyword-match.

6

Timed Activities

Build fluency after understanding, freeing students to think about harder math.
